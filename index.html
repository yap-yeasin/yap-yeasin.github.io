About

I'm someone who loves learning and exploring new ideas. What interests me most is understanding how different things connect, whether it's technology, business, education, psychology, design, or the way people work together. I often find myself exploring topics outside my profession simply because I'm curious and enjoy seeing the bigger picture.

Over the years, I've worked on projects in technology, education, and business, and I've learned that the best solutions rarely come from looking at a problem from only one angle. I enjoy bringing together different perspectives, asking questions, and finding practical ways to turn ideas into reality.

My hands-on side started with small Arduino builds at university and grew into combat robots, drones and autonomous machines with my team, X-Treme. Now I'm learning deep learning, because I want the machines I build to do more than follow instructions.

I value both independent thinking and collaboration. Sometimes I enjoy diving deep into a problem on my own, and other times I enjoy exchanging ideas with others and learning from their experiences. Every project, challenge, and conversation is an opportunity to learn something new.

At the end of the day, I'm driven by curiosity, continuous growth, and the desire to build things that create real value for people.

Read the paper

Unpublished study

Deep learning based artificial vision analysis system using a single camera

A framework for outdoor visual analysis and navigation. It uses YOLO-V4 for object detection, MediaPipe for pose estimation, an LSTM for human movement analysis and DeepFace for emotion analysis. Traffic lights are found with color detection and faces with Haar cascade and KNN, while an ultrasonic sensor on servo motors measures distance. Testing reached 84.83% precision across 95 classes.
